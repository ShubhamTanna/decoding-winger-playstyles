# Final behavioural feature dictionary

This dictionary follows the implemented formulas in `notebooks/04_playstyle_feature_engineering.ipynb` and the saved final `winger_style_feature_specification.csv`, in modelling order. Cell indices are zero-based. The candidate-stage taxonomy is not the final authority.

## Definitions and units

Every input below is an exact column name in the enriched player-club-season table. `Minutes` is spell playing time, not a footballer's combined season total. Notebook 04 cell 6 implements:

- `per90(A, M) = 90 × A / M` when M > 0, otherwise missing.
- `safe_ratio(A, B) = A / B` when B > 0, otherwise missing.
- `per_100(A, B) = 100 × safe_ratio(A, B)`.

Missing inputs propagate. Zero/negative denominators become missing, never zero by default. No clipping, percentage conversion or imputation is implicit. The final saved 20-feature matrix is finite. Shares are raw ratios: a value of 0.4 means 40% when formatted for display; per-100 event rates are not success percentages and need not be bounded by 100. A larger value denotes more of that behaviour, not better performance. Raw units below apply before global z-standardization.

Canonical columns from the initial statistical source include `Minutes`, `Progressive Carries`, `Carries into Penalty Area`, `Take-ons Attempted`, `Progressive Passes`, `Passes Attempted`, `Passes into Penalty Area` and `Total Shots`. Underscore-prefixed groups such as `Touches_*`, `Receiving_*`, `Carries_Carries`, `Pass Types_Crs`, `Total_Att`, `Long_Att` and `Standard_Dist` are donor enrichment fields. See [provenance](provenance.md) for version uncertainties and the source boundary.

Macro families govern family balancing.

## Feature-selection history

Notebook 04 constructs 27 candidates. Initial structural and conceptual screening leaves 21 provisional features. Correlation and redundancy review then excludes `progressive_distance_per_carry`, leaving the final 20 above. Cell 226 records strong overlap with `progressive_carry_share` and `progressive_carries_p90`; exclusion limits overweighting of carrying behaviour. This is an intentional research progression, not a missing 21st final feature. Notebooks 05–08 use the saved 20-feature order.

Related correlated pairs were deliberately retained where they distinguish volume from propensity, such as crosses per 90 versus crossing share. Standardization uses the full spell population; family-balanced sensitivity values multiply z-scores by 1 / sqrt(number of retained features in the macro family). Neither transformation changes the raw formulas.

## Average shot-distance source check

`avg_shot_distance` is copied directly from donor `Standard_Dist` (cell 82). No local unit metadata or conversion establishes its physical unit conclusively. On 5 November 2026, the [Kaggle enrichment source page](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/emrey3lmaz/top-5-league-football-player-stats-2017-2025) exposed no readable data dictionary through the research tool, and the [FBref shooting table](https://fbref.com/en/comps/Big5/shooting/players/Big-5-European-Leagues-Stats) returned HTTP 403. These checks do not establish the historical local export's unit. Label it **source-reported distance (unit unresolved)** until version-specific primary documentation is recovered; do not label it metres or yards or convert it based on assumption.
